# Character identity lock

Use `../assets/se7en-character-reference.png` as the canonical visual source. Preserve identity more strongly than pose, action, layout, or scenery.

## Young man

- Friendly young Chinese man.
- Large round head and compact rounded body, approximately 1:3.5 head-to-body proportion.
- Short neat black hair with a side fringe and one small raised tuft.
- Dot eyes with expression chosen by the action—open and attentive, blinking, half-closed, or peacefully closed when sleeping—plus tiny curved smile, rounded ears, no glasses, and no facial hair.
- Plain white crew-neck long-sleeve shirt, straight dark pants, and simple black shoes.
- Clean rounded limbs, never thin wire or classic stick-figure limbs.

## Cat

- One small chubby light-gray tabby cat.
- Round face, short triangular ears, and dot eyes.
- Three dark forehead stripes with restrained gray back stripes.
- White muzzle and white paws.
- Long curved tail and muted orange collar.
- Preserve the same markings, proportions, and collar in every scene.

## Allowed variation

Change pose, action, expression, props, viewpoint, setting, and composition to match the user's content. Let the cat sit, curl up, walk, observe, interact with a useful prop, ride on the young man's shoulder, rest, or sleep. Use open eyes for alert or curious scenes; use closed eyes only when the cat is actually sleeping or relaxing. Do not repeat a sleeping cat when the scene calls for attention or interaction.

## Non-negotiable exclusions

- No dog, puppy, Border Collie, Shiba, canine silhouette, dog ears, or curled dog tail.
- No additional pets or duplicate cats.
- No glasses on the young man.
- No thin stick-figure anatomy.
- Do not change clothing merely to match the scene.
